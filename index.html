Dissertation & Research Grounding

Curriculum implementation is often a black box. State audits evaluate static paperwork, while teachers face the daily complexity of classroom translation. This portfolio is the artifact base for my dissertation work at National University, which is heading toward a self-study design examining my own practice. The methodology is not settled until my committee meets in 2027, so read that as the direction rather than a finished claim.

By designing lesson-level scaffolding that maps directly to Nevada Career & Technical Education (CTE) indicators, the course map works as a practical model rather than a compliance document. Particular attention goes to AI literacy and ethical scaffolding (Nevada Standard 2.1.2/5.1.4), so students interrogate the technology instead of just consuming it. An article on that approach is currently under review at ACM Inroads.

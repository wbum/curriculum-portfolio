# Four Courses vs. the 2026 CSTA PK–12 Standards — Cross-Course Roll-Up

Aggregates the four per-course CSTA 2026 crosswalks in `03_Teaching`. Those documents are the source of truth and were each verified against course overviews and day plans; this roll-up does not re-judge any status, it only combines them and adds the standards no course table lists.

**Foundational and Specialty are reported separately and never summed.** The 2026 framework treats Foundational (46 standards at HS) as what every graduate should know, and Specialty (S1/S2) as optional deepening. A foundational course is not expected to cover Specialty standards, so a single percentage across all 181 would misstate the picture by design.
